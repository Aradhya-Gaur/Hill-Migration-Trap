# The Hill Migration Trap

## About the book

**The Hill Migration Trap** is an evidence-informed research book examining the causes, consequences, and possible solutions to rural out-migration from the hill villages of Uttarakhand, India.

The book explores how declining agricultural viability, limited employment opportunities, gaps in education and healthcare, changing aspirations, and the growth of tourism have contributed to the depopulation of mountain communities. It also considers the experiences of people who remain in the hills, those who migrate to towns and cities, and households involved in reverse migration.

Rather than treating migration as a single-dimensional problem, the book presents it as a connected social, economic, and environmental challenge. It discusses how village abandonment can affect local economies, traditional knowledge, agricultural land, community institutions, and the long-term resilience of the Himalayan region.

## Topics covered

- Rural-to-urban migration and the depopulation of hill villages
- Agriculture, land abandonment, and changing livelihoods
- Employment, income security, and local economic opportunities
- Education, healthcare, infrastructure, and access to public services
- Tourism and its opportunities and pressures for mountain communities
- Reverse migration and the experiences of returning residents
- Women, youth, and the changing social structure of rural villages
- Sustainable development and community-led solutions
- Policy approaches for strengthening livelihoods in Uttarakhand’s hills
